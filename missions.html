How joining works

Three steps to the operating room

Email Mike

Tell him which mission, your name, your role, and the email you'd like to use. New to ALMA? Attach your CV and a few sentences on why you want to serve.

Get your invitation

Mike invites you to the ALMA Volunteer Hub, where your team, schedule and checklist live.

Get ready to go

Create your profile, upload your passport and license, sign the releases and pay the mission fee.

Email Mike
mike@aloveformissionsabroad.com

Can't travel this year? Sponsor someone who can.

A volunteer sponsorship covers a nurse, tech or interpreter's mission fee so they can serve.
