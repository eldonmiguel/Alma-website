Sign up now

Not sure which mission? Sign up anyway and choose later.

  1. Sign up in the Volunteer Hub with your email, then add your name and role.
  2. Pick a mission, or skip it for now. First time with ALMA? Mike or Ruth confirms your spot. A CV is welcome but optional.
  3. Get ready in the Hub: passport, license, releases and your mission fee donation. CRNAs, physicians (MD/DO) and dentists (DDS/DMD) need a license that's active through the trip; everyone else's is just for our files. Medical volunteers also apply for the required CHG Healthcare Foundation grant at least 60 days before the trip.
