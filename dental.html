The mission

Why Sololá

Sololá sits high in Guatemala's western highlands, above Lake Atitlán, a deep volcanic lake ringed by the Atitlán, Tolimán and San Pedro volcanoes.

This is the heart of Maya Guatemala. Kaqchikel, K'iche' and Tz'utujil communities keep their own languages, hand-woven traje and weekly markets around the lake.

For many highland families, a dentist is far away and costs more than they can pay. Untreated decay, pain and early tooth loss are common, especially in children.

ALMA brings U.S. volunteers to change that. Your team works in a fully equipped clinic alongside Dr. Lucky Caballeros, a Guatemalan dentist who stays with these patients long after you fly home.

The days are full and the work is real: exams, cleanings, restorations, extractions and oral surgery for patients who have waited a long time for care.

Evenings, you're on one of the most beautiful lakes in the world.

The team

Who you'll work with

Partner dentist · Sololá & Panajachel

Dr. Lucky Caballeros

A Guatemalan dentist and ALMA's partner on the ground. Dr. Caballeros runs a dental center in Panajachel, finds and schedules the patients who need care most, works chairside with visiting teams, and stays with those patients after your team goes home.

Oral surgeon

Dr. Trevor Griffitts

A veteran of ALMA's dental and oral surgery missions in Guatemala, where he treats patients and mentors the volunteers who assist him.

Mission volunteer · dental student

Caroline Oswald

Caroline spent years assisting Dr. Griffitts on ALMA missions. The experience led her to dental school.

Ruth Marchorro, ALMA Director, runs every ALMA trip in Guatemala and handles the logistics so your team can focus on patients.
