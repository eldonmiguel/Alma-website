Our dental work in Sololá

Care close to home in the highlands.

In Sololá, ALMA's dental work brings checkups and care to children in communities where a dentist is hard to reach.

$50 covers a child's dental exam and cleaning supplies.

Partnership

Guatemalan and U.S. dentists, side by side.

Dr. Lucky Caballeros

Guatemalan dentist, ALMA partner

Dr. Caballeros, a local Guatemalan dentist, works with ALMA on our dental care. Local partnership means patients have a dentist close to home long after a visiting team leaves.

Dr. Patrick Loftus

Syringa Dental and Implants ↗

Dr. Loftus has served with ALMA as a dentist, bringing dental and implant expertise to our work in Guatemala.

Dr. Trevor Griffitts

Griffitts Facial & Oral Surgery, Hayden, Idaho ↗

Dr. Griffitts, an oral and maxillofacial surgeon, has brought oral surgery care to ALMA trips in Guatemala, working alongside local colleagues.
